EMI means Equated Monthly Installment.

Principal is the original loan amount borrowed.

Interest is the cost charged by lender.

Tenure means repayment duration in months or years.

Lower tenure means higher EMI but lower total interest.

Longer tenure means lower EMI but higher total interest.

Fixed interest rate remains same.

Floating interest rate changes with market.

Prepayment means paying part/full loan early.

Good credit score may help lower rates.
